## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Core Engineering (CoreEng) is a critical foundational organization within Atlassian responsible for developing and maintaining the infrastructure, platforms, and tools that enable all Atlassian product teams to build and run their services reliably at scale. The organization reports directly into the CTO/VP Engineering hierarchy and is deeply integrated with the company's strategic Enterprise Grade and Cell Based Architecture (CBA) initiatives.
