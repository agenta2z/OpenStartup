# bitbucket-sox

You can check more about sox-compliance controls [here](https://hello.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/RELENG/pages/1271107592/HOWTO+enable+Compliance+controls+and+secure+your+repository).

## Main branch compliance

Your main branch (main) must have regulatory compliance control enabled in [Bitbucket](https://bitbucket.org/atlassian/proactive-ai-platform/admin/compliance/).

## Compliance Policy specified for your micros service

Visit [your service in Compass], and select "Compliance" under "apps" in the left hand panel.

## Opsgenie Team and Network Segment specified for your micros service

Visit [your service in Compass], and select "Service Overview" under "apps" in the left hand panel.
